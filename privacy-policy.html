01 Introduction & Scope

Payzue Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Payzue," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our merchants, their customers, website visitors, and other individuals whose data we process in connection with our payment infrastructure platform (the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, the choices available to you, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to information collected through our website at thepayzue.com, our APIs and dashboard, and any other interaction you have with Payzue.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Services.

02 Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Category Examples
Identity & contact data Name, business name, email address, phone number, postal address
Verification & KYC data PAN, GSTIN, certificate of incorporation, bank account details, director/authorised-signatory identity documents
Transaction data Payment amount, timestamp, payment instrument type, UPI VPA (masked), status, and related metadata
Technical & device data IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, log data
Usage data Pages visited, API calls made, dashboard interactions, referral source
Communications Support tickets, emails, call recordings (where legally permitted and disclosed)

We do not collect or store full card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs. Such sensitive payment credentials are handled directly by regulated banking partners and the NPCI network in accordance with PCI-DSS and RBI tokenisation requirements.

03 How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Onboard merchants and verify identity in compliance with KYC and anti-money-laundering obligations;
  • Process, route, and settle payment transactions;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, unauthorised transactions, and other illegal activity;
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries;
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Services, including debugging and analytics;
  • Comply with applicable law, regulation, court orders, and requests from RBI, NPCI, law enforcement, or other governmental authorities; and
  • Send you service-related communications and, where you have consented, marketing communications.

04 Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information on the basis of: (a) performance of a contract with you; (b) compliance with a legal obligation, including RBI, NPCI, and anti-money-laundering requirements; (c) our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Services, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights; and (d) your consent, where required, such as for marketing communications or non-essential cookies.

05 How We Share Information

We may share your information with:

  • Banking & network partners - partner banks, NPCI, and card or UPI networks required to route and settle transactions;
  • Service providers - cloud hosting, identity verification, analytics, customer support, and communication vendors who process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality obligations;
  • Regulators & law enforcement - RBI, NPCI, tax authorities, courts, or other governmental bodies where required by law or to protect our legal rights;
  • Professional advisors - auditors, legal counsel, and insurers, as necessary; and
  • Corporate transactions - an acquirer or successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality commitments.

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember your theme and session preferences, and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality. We currently use first-party functional cookies and limited privacy-respecting analytics; we do not use third-party advertising cookies.

07 Data Retention

We retain personal and transaction information for as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with our legal and regulatory obligations (including RBI recordkeeping requirements, which may require retention of transaction records for a minimum of ten years), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it.

08 Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest, HMAC-signed webhooks, role-based access controls, and continuous monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09 Your Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request erasure of your information, subject to our legal retention obligations;
  • Withdraw consent for processing that is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing; and
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, where applicable law permits.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@payzue.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

10 International Data Transfers

We primarily store and process data within India. Where we engage service providers located outside India, we take reasonable steps to ensure such providers offer an adequate level of protection through contractual safeguards consistent with applicable data protection law.

11 Children's Privacy

The Services are intended for businesses and individuals who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

12 Third-Party Links

Our website and dashboard may contain links to third-party sites, including partner banks and integration marketplaces. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

13 Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will post the revised Policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date and, for material changes, provide additional notice such as an email or dashboard notification.
